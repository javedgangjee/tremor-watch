# Feature 11 validation: Watch face

Every command runs from the repo root. Checks 1 to 8 run on the laptop. Checks 9 to 11 need the watch on USB at /dev/cu.usbmodem101, lying still on a table with this feature's firmware flashed, and a script judges them, so no person needs to look at the watch. Checks 12 to 16 are manual, because only a person can look at the face, tap it, press its button, and hear it. They wait for the user. SCRATCH means the session's scratch folder, and IDFPY means `~/.espressif/tools/python/v6.1/venv/bin/python`. The base commit is 4cfd984.

The limits come from these sources. The contrast limit of 3 to 1 is the large text level of requirement 13. The OKLab limit of 0.10 and the colour vision simulation come from requirement 43, which leaves out the Dose due and Tremor pair since the user chose the design's Dose due purple #3F2559 on 2026-10-23. The limit of 1.1 seconds for a dose to become due comes from check 9 of feature 9. The limits of 10240 bytes of free internal memory and 10 percent of free app partition come from requirement 66. The timings of the face come from requirements 26 and 54 to 59.

In the view cases, O is 1792713600000000, which is 2026-10-23 00:00:00 in microseconds since 1970. The cases call dose_set_clock() with offset O, so timer value 0 is local midnight at the start of Friday 23 October 2026, and "at 16:03" means the timer value 57780000000. The schedule is "Levodopa" at 12:00, 16:00, and 20:00 unless a case says otherwise. A session starts at timer value 0.
