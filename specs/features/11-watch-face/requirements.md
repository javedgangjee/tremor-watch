# Feature 11 requirements: Watch face

## From the project documents

1. The face is built from Waveshare's LVGL example as the plan's Watch face section describes. This comes from Build order step 18.
2. The dose flow has the "Took it" button, the three second lock, and the tone. This comes from Build order step 19.
3. The face runs on the 410 by 502 AMOLED touch screen and is built in LVGL 9.5.0. The screen stays on for the whole session. The board has a clock chip, a battery gauge, and an audio codec, and it has no vibration motor. This comes from the Watch face section and specs/tech-stack.md.
4. Every tap target is at least a third of the screen. There is no swipe, drag, long press, or double tap. This comes from the Watch face section, under Who wears it.
5. Nothing times out while the wearer uses the face. A screen stays open until the button is pressed or five minutes pass. This comes from the Watch face section, under Who wears it.
6. After a dose is logged, the button area ignores taps for three seconds, so a second tap cannot log a second dose. The lock lives in dose_took_it() from core/dose.h, and the dose flow uses its refusal. This comes from the Watch face section and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
7. The wearer never starts or stops a measurement. Tapping "Took it" is the only action, and it is optional. This comes from the Watch face section, under Who wears it.
8. Text is bold, and nothing is smaller than 28 pixels. Colour never carries meaning alone, because every state also has a word and an icon. This comes from the Watch face section, under Who wears it.
9. There is no voice control. Every accepted tap presses the card in and plays one soft tone, and a laptop command can turn the tone off. This comes from the Watch face section, under Who wears it.
10. The face uses light mode, with white cards on a faintly warm background. This comes from the Watch face section, under Look.
11. The type is Red Hat Display bold, converted for LVGL at 28, 40, 56, and 96 pixels only. This comes from the Watch face section, under Look, and specs/tech-stack.md.
12. The time is 96 pixels tall, and the date under it is 28 pixels. The state circle is about 260 pixels wide. This comes from the Watch face section, under Look.
13. The design file sets the colours. Every colour pair must pass a contrast check at the large text level, and the state colours must stay distinct for people who confuse red and green. This comes from the Watch face section, under Look.
14. One large circle in the middle of the Home screen shows the state. A filled circle means the detector gave an answer, and an outline means it did not. The states are Tremor, No tremor, Arm moving, Waiting, and Dose due, with the meanings the plan gives. Dose due turns the circle purple and takes over the circle until the due state ends, and then the detector state returns. This comes from the Watch face section, under The state circle.
15. Every label says Tremor where the design file says Dyskinesia, and the Session figure says "of still time this session". This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
16. No tremor is a filled green circle, in a green clearly different from the "Took it" button. This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
17. The No link and Idle circles and the Bluetooth mark are removed, and the Waiting outline is added. This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
18. Arm moving is drawn as an outline. This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
19. The circle pulses once when the state changes and then holds still. The Logged tick plays once. This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
20. The text sizes follow requirement 12 in place of the design file's 32 and 22 pixels. The clock icon next to "Last" lines is removed. The design file's Today screen becomes the Session screen, and its Status screen becomes the Status list of requirement 30. This comes from the Watch face section, under Where the design file must change.
21. The BOOT button cycles through the screens. The PWR button stays the power button, because holding it turns the board off. This comes from the Watch face section, under Behaviour.
22. No action has a confirm step or an undo. This comes from the Watch face section, under Behaviour.
23. The face shows no severity, off periods, or dyskinesia. This comes from the Watch face section, under Behaviour.
24. Home shows the time and date at the top left, the battery at the top right, and the state circle in the middle with "Last tremor 14:32" under it. A tap anywhere opens Medication. This comes from the Watch face section, under Screens.
25. Medication shows the last dose and the next one due, such as "Levodopa, 16:00". When a dose is due, that line turns purple. The lower half is one large "Took it" button, and only the lower half takes taps. This comes from the Watch face section, under Screens.
26. A tap on "Took it" logs the dose and shows a check mark with "Logged" for three seconds before the face returns to Home. This comes from the Watch face section, under Screens.
27. Session shows the session's share of still time with tremor as one large figure with a plain line under it. Below it sit up to twelve blocks, one for each 10 minutes of the session. A block with under two minutes of still time is drawn hollow. The screen draws the blocks that share_block() in core/share.h gives, and share_block_hollow() says which block is hollow. This comes from the Watch face section, under Screens, and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
28. The share counts only the windows whose number divides by 4, and it leaves out window 0 of each stretch. The two-in-a-row rule changes only what the face shows, and every answer still goes into the share and the recording unchanged. This comes from the Windows and Response time sections.
29. A dose time becomes due at that clock time unless a dose was logged in the hour before it. When a dose becomes due, the circle shows Dose due and the tone plays three times. The due state ends when "Took it" is tapped or fifteen minutes pass, and the watch does not remind again. A dose logged by mistake stays in the recording. This comes from the Dose schedule section.
30. Status lists the battery percentage and voltage, the uptime, the measured sensor rate, the stretch number, the time the last detector run took, whether the recording is on, the firmware version, and the table hash. It is for the build and stays in for the write-up. This comes from the Watch face section, under Screens.
31. Until the clock is set, the face shows dashes for the time and the dose schedule stays off. The dose clock lives in memory only, so after each restart the face shows dashes until the laptop sends the clock command. This comes from the USB recording section and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
32. A D line records a dose taken. It holds the time in microseconds and the scheduled dose time it answers, which is the time that dose_took_it() answers. The state task sends it, and it keeps its pattern in core/recording_format.md, because replay counts a D line that breaks the pattern as a bad line. This comes from the USB recording section, requirement 32 of feature 6, requirement 26 of feature 9, and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
33. The state task writes a TONE line when a dose becomes due, and this feature plays the tone. The TONE command changes the tone setting, the settings store keeps it, and the TONE line gives it as enabled=0 or enabled=1. The face plays the tone only when the setting is on. This comes from the roadmap entry for feature 11.
34. Each part of the watch has one owning task. The state task holds the face state, the share, the dose schedule, and the doses taken. The screen task owns LVGL and only draws what the state task gives it. Only the screen task uses LVGL. This comes from the Firmware design section and specs/tech-stack.md.
35. The screen task starts the display with bsp_display_new() and lvgl_port_add_disp() and the touch with bsp_touch_new() and lvgl_port_add_touch(). It never calls bsp_display_start(), because that function in board support package 2.0.0 adds the SH8601 panel as an RGB panel and corrupts the heap. This comes from specs/tech-stack.md and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
36. Internal memory is tight at boot, and the record queue already moved to PSRAM so that the display's transfer buffer could allocate. This feature checks that the display still starts after it adds the face. This comes from the roadmap entry for feature 11.
37. A medicine name has 1 to 20 characters, and each is a letter, a digit, or one of `-`, `.`, `/`, and `+`. The face's fonts must hold these characters. This comes from requirement 34 of feature 9 and the roadmap entry for feature 11.
38. The icons in the design file are Lucide icons, and they must be converted to LVGL images. Lucide icons have an ISC licence, and Red Hat Display has the SIL Open Font Licence. This comes from specs/tech-stack.md.
39. The design's colours are #111315 for text, #5C6265 for second-level text, #1B2E42 for Tremor, #3F2559 for Dose due, #173A28 for the "Took it" button and Logged, and #C9CDD0 for empty blocks. This comes from specs/tech-stack.md. Requirement 42 keeps the Dose due purple #3F2559, which the user chose on 2026-10-23.
40. Each feature that changes the firmware sets FIRMWARE_VERSION in firmware/main/tasks.h to its own number, so this feature sets it to 11. This comes from specs/tech-stack.md.
41. The core/ library includes no ESP-IDF headers, allocates no memory at run time, builds with -ffp-contract=off, and keeps no static data that changes. Nothing in reference/ changes, nobody retrains the detector or changes its stored numbers, and no code or test assumes a rate of 112.1 readings a second. This comes from the Shared C library section, CLAUDE.md, and the roadmap entry for feature 5.

## Not covered by the project documents

42. The state colours are these. Tremor is a filled #1B2E42 circle, as in the design file. No tremor is a filled #2A7242 circle, which is the No tremor colour of the build spec page's own state list. Dose due is a filled #3F2559 circle, which is the design file's purple, and the due line on Medication uses the same purple. The user chose #3F2559 on 2026-10-23 and rejected the plan's change to #6A3D9A. The user knew that #3F2559 differs from the Tremor navy by about 0.08 in OKLab colour distance for normal vision and about 0.04 to 0.05 for protan and deutan vision, so the two are close for people who confuse red and green. Dose due still has its own word "Dose due" and the pill icon, so colour does not carry the state alone. #3F2559 gives white text a contrast of 13.0 to 1. Arm moving is an outline in the design's Arm moving charcoal #26292B. Waiting is an outline in #5C6265, the design's second-level grey. Both outlines are 8 pixels wide, and their word and icon are #111315 on white. The word and icon on a filled circle are white. The No tremor green and the Waiting outline go beyond the design file, which has neither. The agent decided these on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed them yet. The user decided the Dose due purple on 2026-10-23.
43. The colour checks are these. Each text, icon, outline, and fill must have a WCAG contrast ratio of at least 3 to 1 against the colour it sits on, which is the large text level. The background and the cards are left out, because requirement 10 asks for a faint difference between them. The three filled state colours must differ from each other pairwise, the two outline colours must differ from each other, and No tremor must differ from the "Took it" green, each by an OKLab distance of at least 0.10. The distance is checked for normal vision and for protan and deutan vision, which are simulated with the matrices of Machado and colleagues, 2009, at full strength in linear RGB. The Dose due and Tremor pair is left out of the distance rule, because the user chose the design's Dose due purple #3F2559 on 2026-10-23 knowing that it is close to the Tremor navy. Every other pair and every contrast rule still applies. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
44. The background is #F6F3EE, which is a faint warm white. The cards are #FFFFFF with rounded corners. Home shows its circle and the last tremor line on one white card. Medication shows its two dose lines on one white card above the button. Session shows its blocks on one white card. Status shows its list on one white card. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
45. The Session blocks follow the design file's bars. A block that is not hollow is a #1B2E42 bar from the bottom of its column, whose height is the block's share of the column height, and at least 4 pixels tall so that a block with no tremor still shows. A hollow block is a #1B2E42 outline of 3 pixels around the whole column. #C9CDD0 is not used, because the design used it for idle hours, which this watch does not have, and it gives only 1.6 to 1 on white. Under the blocks the left label gives the local start time of the oldest kept block, or dashes before the clock is set, and the right label says "Now". The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
46. The icons come from Lucide 0.469.0, whose paths match the design file. Tremor uses activity, No tremor uses minus, Arm moving uses vibrate, Waiting uses clock, Dose due uses pill, "Took it" uses check, and Logged uses circle-check-big. The design file has no icon for No tremor or Waiting, so these two take the shapes of the build spec page's state list, a flat line and a clock face. Each icon is 88 pixels square with the design's stroke of 1.5 on Lucide's 24 pixel grid, which is 5.5 pixels, with round caps and joins. Each is an 8-bit alpha image, so LVGL colours it. Logged is split into its ring and its tick, so the tick can be drawn once. The battery is drawn with LVGL shapes as in the design file, with the percentage at 28 pixels. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
47. tools/gen_face_assets.py makes the fonts and the icons, and its output is committed. It converts the static Red Hat Display Bold font of tag 5.0.0 of github.com/RedHatOfficial/RedHatFont, which is commit 6bb1048a6402b0076ea04f42951ec66263cd1437, with lv_font_conv 1.5.3 through npx. The fonts have 4 bits a pixel, compression, and no kerning. The 28, 40, and 56 pixel fonts hold the printable ASCII characters from space to `~`. The 96 pixel font holds only the digits, the colon, the dash, and the space, because only the time uses it. The script draws each icon from its Lucide path with numpy and writes the images as C arrays. It also writes core/face_metrics.h, which holds each font's line height, base line, and the advance width of each character in whole pixels as LVGL rounds it. The font file and its OFL.txt, and Lucide's licence, sit next to the output in firmware/main/assets/. The script runs in the reference venv, which has numpy, and the same run gives the same bytes. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
48. Only Home and the Logged view show the time and date. Medication, Session, and Status show no clock, because the build spec lists the time for Home only, and a 96 pixel time leaves no room for the dose lines above the button. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
49. The time is 24-hour, as the design file's 15:58. The date is a short weekday, the day, and a short month in English, as in "Fri 23 Oct". Before the clock is set, the time shows "--:--" and the date shows "--- -- ---". The last tremor line is "Last tremor HH:MM" with the local time of face_last_tremor(), "Last tremor --:--" when there is a last tremor but no clock, and "Last tremor none" when the session has no last tremor. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
50. Medication has two entries, each a 28 pixel label in #5C6265 and a 40 pixel value. While no dose is due, the entries are "Last dose" and "Next dose". While a dose is due, the first entry is "Dose due" with its value in purple, and the second is "Last dose", as in the design file. A value is the medicine name, a comma, a space, and the time, as in "Levodopa, 16:00". The next time is dose_next(). The last dose time is the local time of the tap that dose_last() gives. The value is "None yet" when the session has no dose, "No schedule" for the next dose when the schedule is off, and the name with "--:--" when the clock is not set. When the schedule has no name, the value is the time alone. The Logged view shows "Next dose" and the next value on one 28 pixel line under the circle. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
51. A text that is wider than its box at its size moves to the next smaller allowed size of its box, and a dose value or a next dose line that is still too wide keeps its time whole and cuts the name with "...". The width of a text is the sum of the advance widths in core/face_metrics.h, which is the width LVGL gives with no kerning. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
52. The Session figure is "Tremor 12%" at 56 pixels, where the percent is share_percent() rounded to a whole number with halves going up, and "Tremor --%" before any still window. The line under it is "of still time this session" at 28 pixels in #5C6265. A block's bar uses share_block_percent() rounded the same way. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
53. Status has nine rows at 28 pixels, each a label at the left and a value at the right. They are Battery as "68%", Voltage as "3.9 V" from the millivolts rounded to tenths, Uptime as "6 h 12 m", Rate as "114.348 Hz" from the stretch's rate in millionths rounded to thousandths, Stretch as the stretch number, Detector as "6.9 ms" from the last run's microseconds rounded to tenths, Recording as "On" or "Off", Firmware as the version number, and Tables as the first 8 digits of the table hash. A value with no data yet, or a battery the power chip does not report, shows "--". The battery on Home shows "--%" in that case. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
54. The BOOT button cycles Home, Medication, Session, Status, and back to Home, which is the order of the design file and of the build spec's Screens list. The Logged view counts as Medication, so BOOT moves it to Session. The button is GPIO0 with the chip's pull-up, as in Waveshare's factory example, and it is active low. The screen task reads it every 20 milliseconds, and a press counts once when two reads in a row find it down after it was up. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
55. A tap is LVGL's clicked event, which comes when the finger lifts on the same object, however long the press lasted, so a slow or frozen press still counts. Home takes a tap anywhere and opens Medication. Medication takes a tap only in its lower half, from y = 251 down, and that tap asks the state task to log a dose. Taps on the upper half of Medication, on the Logged view, on Session, and on Status are ignored. A screen ignores every tap for 1 second after it appears, so a double tap or a shaky second tap on Home cannot open Medication and log a dose. After a "Took it" tap, Medication ignores taps until the state task answers or 1 second passes. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
56. Medication, Session, and Status go back to Home when five minutes pass from the later of the moment the screen appeared and the last tap or BOOT press on it. Every tap counts as use, including a tap the screen ignores. Home never times out. The face does not change screens when a dose becomes due, so nothing moves under the wearer's finger, and the tone and the circle on Home tell the wearer. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
57. When the state task accepts a dose, the Logged view shows for 3 seconds from the moment the screen task learns of it, and then Home shows. The Logged view is the Home layout with a #173A28 circle that holds the ring and tick and the word "Logged". The circle grows in as the design's popIn does, from 90 to 103 to 100 percent over about 1 second, and the tick is uncovered from left to right over about 1.3 seconds, once. When the state task refuses the tap, Medication stays. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
58. The pulse is a ring in the circle's colour, 6 pixels wide, that grows from the circle's size to 120 percent of it while it fades from 45 percent opacity to nothing over 2.4 seconds, as the design's pulseOut does, once for each change of the circle. The press is a scale of the tapped card to 95 percent for 150 milliseconds and back. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
59. The tone plays through the board's ES8311 codec with the board support package's bsp_audio_codec_speaker_init(), at 16000 samples a second in mono. The tap tone is one 660 Hz tone of 120 milliseconds. The dose tone is three 880 Hz tones of 250 milliseconds with 250 milliseconds of silence after each. Each tone has an amplitude of 30 percent of full scale and a fade of 5 milliseconds at each end, so it is soft and does not click. The codec's output volume is 60. With the tone setting off, neither tone plays. core/tone.c fills the samples, and the screen task owns the codec and feeds it 20 milliseconds at a time, so a tone never stops the screen task for more than one round. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
60. The screen rules live in core/screens.c, the colours and layout in core/look.c, the texts the face draws in core/view.c, and the tone samples in core/tone.c, so the laptop tests them. firmware/main/face_draw.c builds and changes the LVGL objects, and firmware/main/tone_out.c drives the codec. Only screen_task.c and face_draw.c call LVGL, and every call of a core/screens.h function happens while the screen task or LVGL's port task holds the display lock. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
61. The layout lives in core/look.c as a table of boxes in pixels for each screen. Each text box is as tall as its font's line height in core/face_metrics.h. Each text and icon box lies inside the screen's rounded shape, with a corner radius of 60 pixels as in the design file. No two text or icon boxes on one screen overlap. The circle is 260 pixels across, and each state word and icon fits inside it. The fills of the "Took it" button reach the screen's edges. If the layout cannot meet these rules with a circle of 260 pixels, implement stops and reports. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
62. The state task builds the view once a second and after each change, with view_build() from core/view.h, and sends it to the screen task. The view holds every text and value of requirements 49 to 53, the shown face state, the blocks, the tone setting, a count of dose tones, and a count of accepted doses. The screen task plays the dose tone when the count of dose tones rises and the setting is on, and shows the Logged view when the count of accepted doses rises. The screen queue holds 4 views and lives in PSRAM, as the record queue does. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
63. A "Took it" tap goes from the screen task to the state task as a new state message with the tap's timer value. The state task calls a new sess_took_it() in core/session.c, which calls dose_took_it() and clears the face's dose due flag when it accepts. On acceptance the state task writes a DOSE line and the D line from rec_dose(), and a FACE line and an F line when the shown state changed. On refusal it writes a DOSE_REFUSED line. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
64. The Status values come through the view. The state task reads the power chip with power_read() every 10 seconds. The pipeline task adds the stretch's rate to each answer, and the state task keeps the last answer's stretch, rate, and run time. The USB task sets a flag that says whether the last line fitted, and Recording is "On" while it is set. The firmware version and the table hash are the build's constants. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
65. The screen task writes these diagnostic lines, and each starts with at_us, the timer value when it was written. SCREEN_START gives whether the display, the touch, and the codec started, and the free and largest free internal memory. SCREEN_VIEW gives the screen, the face state, whether the circle is filled, its colour, the time text, the due flag, the next dose time, the last tremor time, the share, the block count, and which blocks are hollow, each time one of them changes. TONE_PLAY gives the kind of tone and the number of tones. TOUCH keeps its x and y and adds the screen, the target, and whether the tap was accepted. BUTTON gives the new screen. SCREEN_MINUTE keeps its fields and adds the views, the taps, the tones, the stack left, the lowest free internal memory, and the count of texts whose LVGL width differs from the core/face_metrics.h width. STATE_MINUTE adds the battery fields and the counts of accepted and refused doses at its end. Earlier fields keep their places, so the scripts in tests/firmware/ keep working. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
66. The firmware drops every Montserrat size except LVGL's default 14 pixel font, because the face no longer uses them. The app image must leave at least 10 percent of its partition free, and the lowest free internal memory in every SCREEN_MINUTE line must be at least 10240 bytes. If the display does not start or the free memory falls short, implement moves LVGL's small allocations to PSRAM and records the change. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
67. tests/firmware/record_run.py reads FIRMWARE_VERSION from firmware/main/tasks.h in place of its fixed 9, and the tests in tests/firmware/ build their made-up B lines with that value. tests/firmware/face_run.py runs the face's scripted check on the watch, and tests/firmware/tap_run.py guides a person through the taps and the BOOT presses and judges the log. specs/tech-stack.md records the assets tool, the fonts, the icons, the colours, and the two new scripts. core/recording_format.md says that the watch now sends the D line and names the new diagnostic lines. The agent decided this on 2026-10-23, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

## Changes the user made on 2026-10-24

- Home and the Logged view no longer show the top row of time, date, and battery. This goes against the build spec's description of Home, and the user chose it. It changes requirement 48 and the parts of requirement 49 about how the time and date look on the face.
- Home hides the last tremor line while no tremor has been recorded. The text "Last tremor none" no longer shows.
