# Feature 15 requirements: Viewer redesign

"The design" means context/viewer-design/Tremor Log Viewer.dc.html, and "the tokens" means the custom properties in context/viewer-design/colors.css and typography-spacing-fonts.css.

## From the project documents

1. The viewer shows a saved recording, plots the gyroscope readings against time, and marks each window that the watch answered with tremor. Its Record button starts `tools/watch_cli.py record`, a second press stops it as Ctrl-C does, and the viewer then opens the saved file. The viewer never opens the watch's port. This comes from the viewer paragraph in the build spec's USB recording section and the roadmap entry for feature 15.

2. The page takes the design system's colours, type, and controls. Text is in sentence case with no emoji and no exclamation marks. Libre Franklin sets all text except the wordmark, which is Red Hat Display 700, and identifiers such as file names, which are IBM Plex Mono. Counts use tabular figures. Dates read as words, such as `Oct 28, 2026 at 14:28`, in 24-hour time. Cards are white with a 1 px ring and no drop shadow, and only floating layers, such as the readout and the sticky header, get a shadow. Blue is used only for primary actions, links, focus, and selection. Every control shows the focus ring `--shadow-focus`. This comes from context/viewer-design/README.md.

3. A sticky header in `--surface-card` with `--shadow-header` holds the wordmark "Tremor log" in Red Hat Display 700 at 20 px on a 28 px line with -0.015em letter spacing. On the right it holds an "Open recording" secondary button with the folder icon and a "Record" primary button with the play icon. This comes from the design.

4. A title block gives the recording's start as words, in 24 px semibold on a 30 px line, and the file name under it in IBM Plex Mono at 13 px in `--ink-muted`. This comes from the design.

5. One card holds the gyroscope chart. Its head says "Gyroscope" with "°/s" in `--ink-muted`, and it holds a filter chip for each of x, y, and z, each with a 12 by 2 px line in its axis colour, and a segmented control labelled "Chart layout" with the options "Overlaid" and "Stacked". A chip shows or hides its axis. Overlaid draws one plot 420 px high. Stacked draws one plot 150 px high for each shown axis, which is three when all axes show, with the axis name in the left gutter. A 64 px left gutter holds the y tick labels in 12 px `--ink-muted`. Grid lines are `--border` and the zero line is `--border-strong`. The axis colours are `--warning` for x, `--link` for y, and `--success` for z. This comes from the design and the roadmap entry.

6. Under the plots an "Answer" row 28 px high holds one block for each window answer, placed on the time axis. Tremor is filled with `--ink`, arm moving with `--border-strong`, no tremor with `--surface-card` and a 1 px `--border-control` border, and warming up with `--surface-sunken` and a 1 px dashed `--border-control` border. A click selects a block, and the selected block gets the ring `0 0 0 2px var(--surface-card), 0 0 0 4px var(--link)`. The time tick labels follow under the row as h:mm:ss. This comes from the design.

7. A hover readout draws a vertical line in `--ink-secondary` at the pointer and a floating card with `--shadow-card`. The card gives the time as h:mm:ss.s in IBM Plex Mono, the x, y, and z values with a true minus sign and thousands separators in whole degrees per second, and the answer at that time, or "No answer yet" when there is none. The card sits 12 px to the right of the pointer and moves to its left once the pointer passes 60 percent of the plot's width. This comes from the design.

8. A legend row at the bottom of the card gives each answer's 14 px swatch, its name, and its count, in the order Tremor, No tremor, Arm moving, Warming up, and Window span. This comes from the design.

9. The tokens have a light set and a dark set. This comes from colors.css.

10. The boot picker, the summary facts, the cut-short note, the record status line, and the error message stay, restyled with the design system. The summary facts are the boot, the firmware, the reset cause, the stretches, the readings, the window counts, the sensor faults, the bad lines, the dropped bursts, the readings with no stretch, and the windows not placed. This comes from the user's decisions in context/viewer-design/README.md and feature 12's requirement 19.

11. Two vertical lines mark the start and the end of the selected window, in place of the design's shading. They lie 4 seconds apart, because a window covers 4 seconds, as feature 12's requirement 18 says. The legend's "Window span" item describes the lines. The shading of every tremor window's span, from feature 12's requirement 19, goes away. This comes from the user's decisions in context/viewer-design/README.md and the user's request of 2026-10-28.

12. Every rule of features 12 and 14 for reading a file, placing the windows, recording, the six IPC calls, and the window's security settings still holds, except where a requirement below replaces its words. This comes from the roadmap entry for feature 15.

13. A session lasts one to two hours, about 823,000 readings, and feature 12's requirement 24 still holds, so a two hour recording opens and draws within 10 seconds. The page loads nothing remote, as feature 12's requirement 22 says, and Electron and uPlot stay the only two packages, as feature 12's requirement 12 says. This comes from specs/mission.md and those requirements.

14. The three fonts are Red Hat Display (variable), Libre Franklin (variable, roman and italic), and IBM Plex Mono (400, 500, and 600), as latin subsets in woff2, under the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1. This comes from context/viewer-design/README.md.

15. Nothing in firmware/, core/, reference/, tools/, tests/, or context/ changes, and nobody retrains the detector or changes its stored numbers. This comes from CLAUDE.md and the instructions for this run.

## Not covered by the project documents

Each item below is a choice that the build spec, specs/, the design, and the existing code leave open.

16. The viewer keeps uPlot, because SVG paths of 823,000 readings would not draw within 10 seconds. Overlaid is one uPlot chart with the three series, and a hidden axis is a series with show set to false. Stacked is one uPlot chart for each shown axis. Each chart is 420 or 150 CSS px high with 8 px of padding above and below the plotting area, so the top and bottom tick labels show whole. Its y axis is 64 px wide, and its plotting area runs to the card body's right edge. The y axis fits the shown readings in view, and a flat signal still gets a range of 2 degrees per second, as before. The y ticks lie at least 48 px apart. The lines break at each break in the readings with feature 12's gaps hook. The chip of the last shown axis does nothing, so at least one axis always shows. Each chart's box has role img and an aria-label that names its axes and the time span in view. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

17. All charts share one view, which is the time range on the x axis. A drag across any chart zooms every chart to that span, a double click on any chart shows the whole boot in every chart, and the left and right arrow keys move the view of every chart by half its width, in both layouts. The arrow keys do not move the view while the focus is in the boot list, the segmented control, or the answer row, which use them. The charts share the cursor through uPlot's cursor sync, so the hover line shows at the same time in every chart. Switching the layout or a chip keeps the view. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

18. The time axis draws grid lines at the ticks that timeTicks() gives, and the tick labels are a row of HTML text under the answer row at the same ticks. timeTicks() takes the smallest step from 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60, 120, 300, 600, 900, 1800, 3600, 7200, 10800, 21600, 43200, and 86400 seconds that puts the ticks at least 90 px apart. A label is h:mm:ss, with one decimal for a step under 1 second and two for a step under 0.1 seconds, as feature 12 gave. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

19. The answer row is one canvas outside the chart canvases, 28 px of blocks with 4 px above and below for the ring, in a grid with the 64 px "Answer" label. It maps time to x with the shared view and its own width, which equals the plotting area's width. A block covers its window's cell, which is the last second of the window, as feature 12's requirement 18 says. A cell 4 px wide or more is drawn 2 px narrower than the cell, with a radius of 2 px and its full border. A narrower cell is drawn the full width of the cell and at least 1 px, with no gap, no radius, and no side borders, so neighbouring outlined cells join into one band. A block that the view cuts is clipped at the edge. Tremor blocks are drawn last, so they show where cells share a pixel. Sensor faults have no window and are not drawn. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

20. No window is selected when a boot opens. A click on the row selects the window whose cell holds the time under the pointer, and a click on the selected block or on an empty part of the row clears the selection. The row is one element with tabindex 0, role group, and the label "Window answers", and it shows `--shadow-focus` when it has the keyboard focus. With the focus there, the right and left arrow keys select the next or the previous window in time. With no selection, the right arrow selects the first window whose cell ends after the view's start, and the left arrow the last window whose cell starts before the view's end. Home and End select the boot's first and last window, and Escape clears the selection. When the selected cell lies outside the view, the view moves to centre it and keeps its width, within the boot. A line at the right of the legend row, with role status, gives the selected window as text, such as "Window 5 of stretch 0: tremor, from 0:00:05.3 to 0:00:09.3", and is empty with no selection. The selection stays through layout and chip changes and is cleared when another boot or file opens. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.

The legend's "Window span" item has a 14 px swatch with no fill and a 1 px `--link` line at its left and right edges, and no count. windows.js keeps tremorSpans() and load.js still gives each boot's tremorSpans, because feature 12's check 1 cases 14, 15, and 21 check the window placement with them, but the page draws no span. 23. formatValue(value, decimals) rounds to the given decimals, 0 by default, puts a comma between each group of three digits, and puts the minus sign U+2212 before a negative value. A value that rounds to zero gets no minus sign. The readout gives whole degrees per second. The y tick labels use formatValue() with decimalsFor(step), which is 0 for a step of 1 or more, 1 for a step of 0.1 or more, and 2 below that, so ticks of a small range do not read the same. Canvas text cannot ask for tabular figures, so the y tick labels in the charts, which are right-aligned, use the font's own figures. The summary's counts keep feature 12's comma grouping. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
