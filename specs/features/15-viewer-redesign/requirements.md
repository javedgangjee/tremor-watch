# Feature 15 requirements: Viewer redesign

"The design" means context/viewer-design/Tremor Log Viewer.dc.html, and "the tokens" means the custom properties in context/viewer-design/colors.css and typography-spacing-fonts.css. "The v2 design" means context/viewer-design/Tremor Log Viewer v2.dc.html, which the user made on 2026-10-29. Requirement 37 gives what the v2 design changes, and where it and an earlier requirement differ, requirement 37 holds.

## From the project documents

1. The viewer shows a saved recording, plots the gyroscope readings against time, and marks each window that the watch answered with tremor. Its Record button starts `tools/watch_cli.py record`, a second press stops it as Ctrl-C does, and the viewer then opens the saved file. The viewer never opens the watch's port. This comes from the viewer paragraph in the build spec's USB recording section and the roadmap entry for feature 15.

2. The page takes the design system's colours, type, and controls. Text is in sentence case with no emoji and no exclamation marks. Libre Franklin sets all text except the wordmark, which is Red Hat Display 700, and identifiers such as file names, which are IBM Plex Mono. Counts use tabular figures. Dates read as words, such as `Oct 28, 2026 at 14:28`, in 24-hour time. Cards are white with a 1 px ring and no drop shadow, and only floating layers, such as the readout and the sticky header, get a shadow. Blue is used only for primary actions, links, focus, and selection. Every control shows the focus ring `--shadow-focus`. This comes from context/viewer-design/README.md.

3. A sticky header in `--surface-card` with `--shadow-header` holds the wordmark "Tremor log" in Red Hat Display 700 at 20 px on a 28 px line with -0.015em letter spacing. On the right it holds an "Open recording" secondary button with the folder icon and a "Record" primary button with the play icon. This comes from the design. Requirement 37 replaces the size of the header and the wordmark.

4. A title block gives the recording's start as words, in 24 px semibold on a 30 px line, and the file name under it in IBM Plex Mono at 13 px in `--ink-muted`. This comes from the design. Requirement 37 removes the title block.

5. One card holds the gyroscope chart. Its head says "Gyroscope" with "°/s" in `--ink-muted`, and it holds a filter chip for each of x, y, and z, each with a 12 by 2 px line in its axis colour, and a segmented control labelled "Chart layout" with the options "Overlaid" and "Stacked". A chip shows or hides its axis. Overlaid draws one plot 420 px high. Stacked draws one plot 150 px high for each shown axis, which is three when all axes show, with the axis name in the left gutter. A 64 px left gutter holds the y tick labels in 12 px `--ink-muted`. Grid lines are `--border` and the zero line is `--border-strong`. The axis colours are `--warning` for x, `--link` for y, and `--success` for z. This comes from the design and the roadmap entry. Requirement 37 replaces the plot heights and the place of the axis name.

6. Under the plots an "Answer" row 28 px high holds one block for each window answer, placed on the time axis. Tremor is filled with `--ink`, arm moving with `--border-strong`, no tremor with `--surface-card` and a 1 px `--border-control` border, and warming up with `--surface-sunken` and a 1 px dashed `--border-control` border. A click selects a block, and the selected block gets the ring `0 0 0 2px var(--surface-card), 0 0 0 4px var(--link)`. The time tick labels follow under the row as h:mm:ss. This comes from the design.

7. A hover readout draws a vertical line in `--ink-secondary` at the pointer and a floating card with `--shadow-card`. The card gives the time as h:mm:ss.s in IBM Plex Mono, the x, y, and z values with a true minus sign and thousands separators in whole degrees per second, and the answer at that time, or "No answer yet" when there is none. The card sits 12 px to the right of the pointer and moves to its left once the pointer passes 60 percent of the plot's width. This comes from the design. Requirement 37 replaces the font of the time and the values shown.

8. A legend row at the bottom of the card gives each answer's 14 px swatch, its name, and its count, in the order Tremor, No tremor, Arm moving, and Warming up. Two items with no count follow, "Tremor start and end" and "Window span". This comes from the design and the user's decisions of 2026-10-28 in context/viewer-design/README.md, which add the item for the tremor lines. Requirement 37 removes the legend row.

9. The tokens have a light set and a dark set. This comes from colors.css.

10. The boot picker, the summary facts, the cut-short note, the record status line, and the error message stay, restyled with the design system. The summary facts are the boot, the firmware, the reset cause, the stretches, the readings, the window counts, the sensor faults, the bad lines, the dropped bursts, the readings with no stretch, and the windows not placed. This comes from the user's decisions in context/viewer-design/README.md and feature 12's requirement 19. Requirement 37 replaces the list of summary facts.

11. In every plot of both layouts, one vertical line marks the start and one the end of each tremor episode. These lines take the place of the design's shading and of the shading of every tremor window's span from feature 12's requirement 19. An episode is the union of the 4-second signal spans of the tremor windows, joined as tremorSpans() joins them under feature 12's requirement 18. So the start line lies at the start of the first tremor window's span, and the end line at the end of the last one's. The lines have no text on the plot, and a legend item names them. The two lines of the selected window stay, 4 seconds apart, because a window covers 4 seconds. They are dashed and grey, so they never look like the tremor lines or the blue y trace, and the legend's "Window span" item shows the same dashed grey style. Requirement 37 adds a shade to each episode and makes the selection lines `--ink-secondary`.

12. Every rule of features 12 and 14 for reading a file, placing the windows, recording, the six IPC calls, and the window's security settings still holds, except where a requirement below replaces its words. This comes from the roadmap entry for feature 15.

13. A session lasts one to two hours, about 823,000 readings, and feature 12's requirement 24 still holds, so a two hour recording opens and draws within 10 seconds. The page loads nothing remote, as feature 12's requirement 22 says, and Electron and uPlot stay the only two packages, as feature 12's requirement 12 says. ## Not covered by the project documents

Each item below is a choice that the build spec, specs/, the design, and the existing code leave open.

16. The viewer keeps uPlot, because SVG paths of 823,000 readings would not draw within 10 seconds. Overlaid is one uPlot chart with the three series, and a hidden axis is a series with show set to false. Stacked is one uPlot chart for each shown axis. Each chart is 420 or 150 CSS px high with 8 px of padding above and below the plotting area, so the top and bottom tick labels show whole. Its y axis is 64 px wide, and its plotting area runs to the card body's right edge. In Overlaid the y axis fits the shown readings in view. In Stacked all the plots share one y range, so each plot has the same scale, which the user chose on 2026-10-28, as context/viewer-design/README.md records. The shared range runs from the lowest to the highest reading of every shown axis in view, with 5 percent of that height added above and below, as before. A flat signal still gets a range of 2 degrees per second, and no readings in view give -1 to 1. The range is found again whenever the view or the shown axes change. The y ticks lie at least 48 px apart in Overlaid and at least 24 px apart in Stacked, so that each 150 px plot shows at least two labels. How the shared range is found and the 24 px spacing are the agent's choices. The lines break at each break in the readings with feature 12's gaps hook. The chip of the last shown axis does nothing, so at least one axis always shows. Each chart's box has role img and an aria-label that names its axes and the time span in view. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet. Requirement 37 replaces the heights of 420 and 150 px with 340 and 120 px.

The arrow keys do not move the view while the focus is in the boot list, the segmented control, or the answer row, which use them. The charts share the cursor through uPlot's cursor sync, so the hover line shows at the same time in every chart. Switching the layout or a chip keeps the view. The agent decided this on 2026-10-28, and the user has not reviewed it yet.
