### The look against the design

The stage made captures of the made-up recording and of recordings/tremor-2026-10-28-142813.txt.gz, which is the design's own 30-second example, in light and dark and in both layouts, and opened each. The header, the title block, the chips, the segmented control, the Answer row, the legend, and the readout card match the design. These places differ or read poorly.

- In stacked mode each plot fits its own readings, so a plot of 150 px often gets one y label. The x plot of the made-up file, from about −165 to 165, and the x plot of the design's example get only "0", because a tick needs 48 px and the next ticks, at 200 or 500, fall outside the range. This meets requirement 16 as written, which asks for 48 px between ticks and not for two labels. It reads poorly, because the plot has no scale.
- The made-up file's z readings, and its y readings away from the sway, are noise of about 0.3 degrees per second. Fitted to its own range, that noise fills the whole 150 px plot as a solid band labelled "0.0", and in a zoomed view it draws as waves of full height while the readout gives 0. The two hour file's y and z plots look the same. This meets requirements 16 and 23 as written, because the 2 degrees per second floor holds only for a flat signal, as it did in feature 12. It reads poorly, because noise looks like large motion, and one plot reads "0" while the next reads "0.0". The design draws every plot on one fixed range of ±1100.
- With no start in the file name or a CLOCK line, the title block holds only the small grey file name, with no heading, which requirement 28 allows.
- The y line and the selection lines are both `--link` blue, as requirements 5 and 21 say. In Overlaid, where y moves, the two window lines are harder to tell from the y trace.
