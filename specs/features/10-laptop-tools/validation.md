# Feature 10 validation: Laptop tools

Every command runs from the repo root. Checks 1 to 9 run on the laptop. Checks 1, 8, and 9 need no toolbox, check 2 needs PADS in data/pads/, and checks 3 to 7 need the toolbox in the reference venv and the laptop build of replay. Checks 10 and 11 need the watch on USB at /dev/cu.usbmodem101, lying still on a table with feature 9's firmware flashed, and a script judges them, so no person needs to look at the watch. No check is manual. SCRATCH means the session's scratch folder, and IDFPY means `~/.espressif/tools/python/v6.1/venv/bin/python`.

The limits come from these sources. The probability limit of 0.0001, the probability margin of 0.0001 around the cut-off, the margin of 0.005 around 50 for the low-frequency sum, and the peak margin of 0.01 percent come from Build order step 5, as tests/pads/pads_match.py uses them. The grid limit of 1e-9 seconds comes from tests/converter/make_cases.py. The clock limit of 1 second comes from check 9 item 6 of feature 9. The toolbox's agreement and the share have no limit, because the build spec asks only that they be reported.

The expected toolbox counts of check 7 come from a planning run on 2026-10-23. It ran the toolbox call of requirement 29 on the committed logs and compared toolbox window k with the watch's W line for window 4k from k = 1 on.
