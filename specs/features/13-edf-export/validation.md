# Feature 13 validation: EDF export

Every command runs from the repo root after `cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build`. PY is `reference/.venv/bin/python`, and edfio 0.4.18 must be installed in that venv. SCRATCH is a new folder in the session's scratch folder. Checks 1 to 4 need no watch. Check 5 needs the watch on USB at /dev/cu.usbmodem101 with the Tremor log firmware. Check 6 is manual and waits for the user.

The limits come from these sources. The rounding limit of 1/64 degree per second is half of one digital step of 1/32, as requirement 23 says. The onset limit of 1e-9 seconds allows only for edfio turning exact decimal text into a float. Every other number is exact.

## Terms

- "Export F" means `PY tools/export_edf.py F --out-dir D` with a new temporary folder D, run in a subprocess, or main() with the same arguments.
- "Replay of F" means `build/core/replay --recording - --samples S` on the lines of F without the laptop time. For each boot and stretch of S the samples are x, y, and z in degrees per second.
- "Digital of x" means numpy.clip(numpy.rint(32 × x), -32768, 32764).
- "The test's TAL reader" is a small reader in the test file that splits each data record of the raw bytes into its signals, reads the first TAL of the annotation signal as the record's start with decimal.Decimal, and lists the other TALs of each record. It imports nothing from tools/.
- "The test's line reader" reads the B, S, W, F, D, and CLOCK lines of a recording with its own regular expressions, keeps the last S line of each stretch and the last W line of each stretch and window, and imports nothing from tools/.
- The first record time R0 of a boot is the t0 of its first replay stretch with samples, in microseconds.
